Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to all mobile applications and games (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that were created by WhyNutt Studio (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service with optional In-App Purchases. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device
  • Purchase transaction history and identifiers (e.g., product IDs, purchase tokens, order IDs) when you make in-app purchases

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

In-App Purchases & Financial Data

The Application offers optional in-app purchases. All financial transactions, payments, and sensitive billing details (such as credit card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses) are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing (Google LLC).

The Service Provider does not collect, store, or have access to your payment card details or financial information. We only receive confirmation tokens, order IDs, and product identifiers from Google to validate your purchases and grant you the digital goods or services purchased.

The Application uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect usage telemetry, performance metrics, and crash reports (such as stack traces, device state, and pseudonymous identifiers). This data helps us analyze player behavior, diagnose app stability issues, and improve user experience.

Analytics and crash data may be exported to and processed using Google BigQuery and Looker Studio for business intelligence, aggregated reporting, and data visualization purposes on Google Cloud infrastructure.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

For a better experience, while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that the Service Provider request will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.
